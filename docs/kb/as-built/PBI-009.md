## How to extend

- Register a function with `registerFunction` in `apps/web/lib/command-palette/function-router.ts` (`code`, `panelId`, `arg`: `symbol` \| `query` \| `none`, `title`)
- Map new panels in `panel-registry.ts` first; DES content replaces the `des` placeholder in PBI-020
- Do not hard-code extra function codes in the parser — it reads the router
- Recents key `meridian.command.recents.v1`; do not fork a second recents store
