## How to extend

- New P0 journey: add a tagged spec under `apps/web/e2e/regression` (`@TC-nnn-xx` `@P0`) and keep helpers in `e2e/helpers`. Path A Playwright membership is asserted by `REQUIRED_REGRESSION_E2E` in `scripts/traceability.mjs`.
- New seed stage: append a `seedAllStepSchema` value (do not reorder existing stages) and extend `evaluateFullSeedCounts`.
- Do not index `docs/` or `docs/kb/` into news RAG. Do not hard-code policy numbers in the runbook.
